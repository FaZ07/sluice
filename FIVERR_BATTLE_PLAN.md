## The wedge test — run BEFORE publishing (10 min, do this in the Fiverr search bar)

For each gig's main keyword, search Fiverr and check page 1:
- **Enterable niche:** at least 2–3 page-1 gigs have <50 reviews → new gigs can rank. Publish as speced.
- **Walled niche:** page 1 is all 500+ review sellers → sharpen the title one level narrower (e.g. "fix python error" → "fix python selenium script error") and re-test.
Record what you see; it also tells you the going prices — position Basic ~20% under the median page-1 Basic.

## UNIVERSAL RANKING PLAYBOOK (applies to all 3 — the "deep tricks")

**Search & ranking**
1. **Keyword goes first in the title** (right after "I will"). The gig URL slug is generated from the first title you save and is **permanent** — get the title final before publishing.
2. **Tags:** 5 per gig — 1 head term + 3 mid + 1 long-tail (specified per gig below). Don't repeat the exact same tag set across gigs; you want each gig catching different searches.
3. **Fill EVERY metadata checkbox** in the gig form (programming language, expertise, purpose…). These are search *filter facets* — most sellers skip them, and filtered searches have far fewer competitors.
4. **FAQ is indexed.** Each FAQ below works secondary keywords in naturally. Don't skip them.
5. **Description first 2 lines appear in search preview** — every description below opens with the keyword + the differentiator, not a greeting.
6. **The first 14 days are everything.** New gigs get a temporary visibility boost. Bad early CTR/conversion sinks the gig semi-permanently. That's why thumbnails, pricing, and the free-sample closer below are not optional.

**Click-through (thumbnail rules — biggest CTR lever)**
7. One consistent brand look across all 3 gigs (page looks professional): dark navy background `#0F172A`, teal accent `#5EEAD4`, huge white text readable at 200px width, 3–5 words max. Per-gig art directions below. No stock clip-art, no Fiverr-green, no tiny screenshots.
8. **Add a video to every gig** (<60s; Fiverr's own guidance: video ≈ 40% more engagement). Scripts below. Show the deliverable in the first 5 seconds — buyers preview without sound, so burn in captions.
9. Use the 2 **PDF gallery slots** almost nobody uses: a 1-page sample deliverable / mini case study per gig. Free extra proof surface.

**Conversion (turning views into orders)**
10. **Risk-reversal closer in every description**: a free micro-sample ("message me first"). It moves the buyer into chat — and pre-order chats convert several times better than cold orders, plus you get to qualify scope before the clock starts.
11. **Decoy pricing:** make Standard the obvious best value (Basic exists to anchor cheap; Premium exists to make Standard look reasonable). Structures below follow this.
12. **Requirements form is your Success-Score armor.** Never start work without complete requirements; if a buyer demands something out of scope, use the Resolution Center to adjust scope — **never cancel** (cancellations crater Success Score and ranking). If a cancellation is unavoidable, route it through Fiverr Support so it may not count against you.
13. **Delivery buffer:** promise 2 days when you can do it in hours, then deliver early. "Delivered ahead of schedule" is a visible trust signal; a single late delivery is a lasting scar.

**Behavior (ranking factors you control daily)**
14. **Response time <1h, every message, 7 days.** Install the app, notifications on. This is both a ranking input and the whole mechanism of the urgency gig.
15. **Stay online during India evening + US morning overlap (roughly 6pm–1am IST)** — the "Online sellers" filter is heavily used and only shows people currently active.
16. **Enable Briefs** (Growth & Marketing) with a wide budget range and the same keywords as your tags; respond to every matched brief with the free-sample offer.
17. **External traffic — targeted only.** A LinkedIn post to people who might buy is fine; blasting the link to classmates who click and bounce degrades the gig's view→order conversion metric. No junk traffic.

**Review engine (first 5 reviews decide the gig's fate)**
18. Over-deliver something small and visible on every early order (extra rows cleaned, a bonus README, 1 extra bug fixed) + deliver early.
19. In the delivery message, name the service naturally: "Your **Python bug fix** is complete — all tests passing." Buyers echo your wording in reviews, and keyworded reviews boost search ranking.
20. One compliant review line, nothing more: *"If this was useful, a review helps my new profile a lot."* Never offer anything in exchange (ban risk), never ask twice.
21. **Price ladder:** after 5 reviews raise prices ~40%; after 15 reviews raise again to the market rate in each spec below.

**Repeat-buyer hook (retention is a ranking factor AND the income)**
22. Every delivery ends with a specific repeat offer: cleaning → "same dataset re-cleaned monthly"; chatbot → "monthly document refresh + tuning"; bug fix → "I keep your project on file — future fixes are faster."

**Analytics (when to intervene)**
23. Check gig Analytics daily during the first 14 days. Heuristics: impressions but **CTR under ~1%** for 10+ days → thumbnail/title problem, edit them. Clicks but **no orders/messages after ~200 clicks** → pricing/description problem. Caution: editing a gig can temporarily dip its ranking — change one thing at a time, only with data, never in week 1.
