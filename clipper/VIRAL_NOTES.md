# Viral clipping notes (researched 2026-11-19)

Read this before a clipping session. Each claim is labelled with its strength:
**[official]** = platform or Content Rewards docs, **[repeated]** = several
sources agree (but most of them are vendor blogs), **[single]** = one source,
**[hype]** = unproven or selling something. We couldn't reach Reddit (403
errors), so first-hand clipper reports are thin. GTA 6 came out on the day this
was written, so expect a flood of competing clips.

## The 10 rules the clipper is built around
1. Read each brief line by line. The brief is the only rulebook. [official]
2. Submit the post link **within 30 minutes** of posting, **to one campaign only**. [official]
3. **Open on the payoff or reaction** and cut the setup. Put one line of question text near the top of the frame. [repeated]
4. **18-45 s**, with every pause and "uhh" cut. [repeated] (`clip.py` warns outside this range)
5. 9:16 at 1080x1920, no black bars, facecam readable (a small facecam scaled up looks pixelated). [repeated]
6. Short bold captions, kept **out of the bottom third** where the Shorts buttons and title sit. [repeated] (`clip.py` puts them at y<=1180)
7. **No GTA radio music** (Content ID) and **no story spoilers or cutscenes** (Rockstar takes these down). [repeated, secondary sources]
8. English titles written like a search, with the streamer's name and topic ("Dylan Page GTA 6 police chase"). Post in US evening hours, which is early morning IST. [single / official on timing]
9. Add real editing (cuts, hook, captions, zooms). Since YouTube's 2026-10-01 change, Shorts channels that mostly re-upload other people's video get less reach. [official via secondary coverage]
10. Grow organically. **Never**: buy views, likes or followers, use bots, VPN farms, buy a page, set the channel country to USA (Google says that setting doesn't affect recommendations), or reuse one edit across campaigns. [official]

## Picking moments
- Pick moments a stranger understands with no context: a readable reaction, a rage moment with a funny beat, skill with something at stake, disbelief, a real glitch. Skip inside jokes and build-up with no payoff. [repeated]
- Look for chat spikes and audio spikes. [single]
- During a launch week, posting fast wins. The "post within 30-90 minutes" figure comes from a tool vendor with no data [hype], but the general direction is repeated.

## Hook formats for GTA 6
- "Watch his face at 0:06"
- "Chat had to explain what just happened"
- "He did NOT expect the cops to do this"
- Avoid "insane moment" and "you won't believe this". [single]

## Posting
- 1-3 clips a day at the start. Sources disagree; "25-50 a day across 10 accounts" is agency hype.
- Cross-posting the same edit to TikTok and Reels is fine, but submit it to only one campaign. [official]
- Publish time doesn't change a clip's long-term reach, but posting when US viewers are awake helps the first hour. [official]

## The tier-1 audience problem (main risk for us)
Many campaigns need 30-50%+ of viewers from the US, UK, Canada or Australia (KreekCraft: 40%). In one example, 10K views at 15% tier-1 was **rejected** while 3K views at 60% tier-1 was paid. [single] The only fair levers are English everything, US-evening posting, and moments Western viewers get. Dylan Page and Dom have no country rule, so they're the realistic targets.

## Realistic first 2 weeks
Most clips get under 1,000 views in the first month, and one or two may reach 10K-50K by luck. [single, the only source with numbers] With 3K-10K view minimums, expect **little or no payout in weeks 1-2**. The stop rule stands: under 10,000 paid views by 2026-12-03 means stop and park the idea.

## Why clips get rejected or get zero views
- **Rejected** [official]: missed a brief rule, no disclosure, views that look botted, reused edit, late submission, wrong campaign, account not verified, wrong country audience.
- **Zero views:** never made it into YouTube's first test audience, usually because of black bars, the wrong aspect ratio or a slow opening. [vendor]

## Not adopted (no evidence)
- Stacked layout vs blurred-fit "+8-15% / 1.7x" claims [hype]: `fit` stays the default, so try `stack` and compare your own numbers.
- Loop endings [hype]: optional.
- Word-highlight captions (one coloured word per block) [single]: not built yet. Add it only if our own numbers say captions are the bottleneck.
