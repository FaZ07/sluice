## 5. Getting the FIRST real order (the honest playbook)

Fiverr is inbound: typical first order takes **1–4 weeks** even with a good
gig. If money is urgent, run outbound in parallel — that's Upwork (GIG.md §2
template, 5 proposals/day on lead-gen jobs). Outbound converts in days;
inbound compounds for months. Do both.

Daily, non-negotiable (fits one SPINE block):
1. **Respond to every message within 1 hour.** Install the Fiverr app,
   notifications ON. Response time is a ranking factor and new-gig boost is
   wasted on slow responders.
2. **Enable Briefs** (Selling → toggle "Get briefs") with the same keywords
   as your tags. Reply to every matched brief with the sample offer.
3. **5 Upwork proposals** with the 20-row-sample closer.
4. First 5 orders: over-deliver (~10% extra rows, deliver early), then one
   polite line: "If the data worked for you, a review helps me a lot."

Never: buy reviews, swap reviews, accept personal-data/login-scraping gigs,
or cancel an order you could rescue (cancellations crater the Success Score —
scope hard via the requirements form instead).

## 6. When money actually arrives (plan around this)

- Fiverr takes **20%** of every order.
- New-seller funds clear **14 days after delivery is accepted**.
- Withdraw via Payoneer → Indian bank (or PayPal). Set this up at signup,
  not at first withdrawal.
- So: order today → deliver tomorrow → cash in bank in ~2.5 weeks.
  **Fiverr is this-month money, not this-week money.** Upwork + the free
  sample is the fastest path to a real paid order.
